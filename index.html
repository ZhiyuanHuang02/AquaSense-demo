The design question

What if a water test explained itself?

Laboratory analysis is essential, but preliminary screening can still be difficult to access, transport, and interpret. AquaSense begins at that gap.

Our concept combines a freeze-dried, cell-free sensing reaction with a simple visual scale and a closed cartridge. The goal is not to replace certified analysis. It is to help communities decide when and where follow-up testing is most urgent.

From sample to signal

Five deliberate steps.

The interaction is short; the design decisions behind it are not. Each step reduces ambiguity for the person collecting the sample.

01

Collect

Fill the marked dropper from the water source.

02

Release

Add the sample to a sealed, single-use cartridge.

03

Recognize

A metal-responsive regulator interacts with the target in a cell-free reaction.

04

Translate

The circuit converts molecular recognition into a visible color response.

05

Decide

Compare against the printed guide and identify the appropriate next step.
