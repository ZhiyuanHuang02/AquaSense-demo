05 · Open questions

The most useful result may be the next question.

Can the proposed regulator distinguish lead in complex samples?
Selectivity must be measured across individual interferents, mixtures, realistic pH and hardness ranges, and blinded environmental samples.
Will the freeze-dried reaction remain stable during transport?
Accelerated aging, temperature cycling, humidity exposure, packaging permeability, and rehydration consistency would all need to be characterized.
Can people interpret the result in uncontrolled lighting?
A larger study should include diverse lighting, color-vision differences, literacy levels, languages, and comparison with an optional phone-based reader.
What happens after a priority result?
The device is only responsible if a locally funded confirmation, communication, and remediation pathway exists before screening begins.
